Comms living graph prototype

Comms as a living graph: seats as nodes, messages as signals

For: you. Replaces: the Comms graph. Decision: is this the feel you meant? It has no play button and no timeline. Live, a signal travels only at the moment a message is sent.

This prototype has no live feed, so it fires 48 hours of real message traffic (who wrote to whom and when; the wording is placeholder text) (read 9 November 2026, 21:55) in order at speed, then starts again. That is only so you can see it move.

Waiting on a reply

    Just said

      node = a seat in session; bigger = more trafficlink brightness = how recently they spoke moving light = one message, in the sender's colouramber light held at a node = that seat owes a reply small orbiting dots = a seat's subagents (illustration only)

      What it shows

      • Who is in session and who they talk to. Seats that talk a lot pull together; quiet ones drift out.
      • A conversation as it happens. The signal leaves the sender, travels, and the recipient lights up.
      • Who is waiting on whom. An ask leaves an amber light parked at the seat that owes the reply. It clears when they answer. The list beside the graph says the same in words.

      What is real and what is not

      • Real: the seats, every message, who sent it to whom, its first line, and which were asks.
      • Guessed: "answered". Few messages record what they reply to, so an ask clears here when the recipient next writes back to the sender. Ticket #1663 fixes that at the source.
      • Illustration: the subagent dots. The hub does not record when a seat starts a helper agent. A seat's live output does carry it, so it could be recorded; nobody has checked how reliably.
